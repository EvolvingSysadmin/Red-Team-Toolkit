# Collection

Reaching the objective: maintaining access and demonstrating impact through data access and exfiltration.

## Why It Matters

Collection is where an engagement proves impact. Showing that a persistence mechanism survives a reboot, or that sensitive data can be staged and removed, makes the risk concrete to a client rather than theoretical.

## How I Use It

Within the rules of engagement, I demonstrate impact rather than cause harm: enough persistence and data access to prove the risk, documented for the report.
